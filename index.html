About me

From fish in the field to the genome.

I am an aquatic biologist trained in ichthyology, and I am fascinated by the evolutionary mechanisms that shape fish diversity. My work so far has taken me from pikeperch spawning grounds in the Landes to Arctic charr in Icelandic lakes, combining fieldwork, ecological modelling and genomics.

After a Master's in Aquatic Biology, I am now completing a second Master's in genomics and evolution to strengthen the molecular and computational side of my research. I am particularly interested in how genetic architecture and ecology interact during adaptive divergence.
